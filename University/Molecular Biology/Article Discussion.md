Lipoprotein Cofactors Located in the Outer Membrane Activate Bacterial Cell Wall Polymerases
Paradis-Bleau, et al. 

How to read:
1. Abstract, skim intro
2. Figures, tables and graphs
3. Conclusion
4. The Rest (technical data etc.)


Abbreviations and Keywords:
- PG: Peptidoglycan exoskeleton
- PBPs: Penicillin-binding proteins, polysaccharide polymerases. Class A, bifunctional, PGT and TP activity. Class B, mono-functional, TP activity
- LpoA/B: Lipoproteins outer membrane A/B, cofactors for class A PBP
- Envelope: the whole multi-layered boundary enveloping the bacteria cytoplasm. Cell wall is a specific layer of the envelope which primarily keeps the shape and prevents bursting

**What to look for:**

- Why is the study important?
	- Bacteria is surrounded by PG exoskeleton which is produced by PBPs
	- PBPs are well studied as targets of penicillin and related antibiotics
	- **However**, ==🟡little is known== about how PBPs build PG layer in vivo
	- ==🟢Understanding how PBPs build PG may create new targets for antibiotics==
	
- Which questions are they trying to answer?
	- Do PBP-complexes promote PG synthesis by providing access to substrate, or do they also facilitate and/or regulate PBP-activity via "critical accessory factors"
	- Which are some of the cofactors important for PG synthesis?
	- How to cofactors LpoA and LpoB promote PG synthesis?
	
- What did they find out?
	- LpoA and LpoB are protein cofactors essential for in vivo class A PBP.
	- Forms trans-envelope Lpo-PBP complexes (PBP1a and PBP1b)
	- In vitro, LpoB promote PG synthesis via stimulating glycan chain polymerization
	- PBP accessory proteins central role in PG synthesis and may potentially be targets for antibiotics.
	- 
	
- How did they prove this experimentally?
	- Synthetic lethal screens of *E. coli* LpoA and B
	- PBP1a and PBP1b important *E. coli*. Screen for mutants lethal with the loss of PBP1b function (slb). 
	- Chromosomal deletions of lacZ and ponB necessary for PBP1b function. Unstable plasmid contained the lost genes and induced by P_lac
	-

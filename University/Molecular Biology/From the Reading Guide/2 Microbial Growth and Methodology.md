Doubling time or generation time is the time it takes for a population to double in size and is important for knowing how fast a strain of bacteria can grow. The doubling time for different species can vary from less than 20 minutes to more than 2 weeks.
A growth curve can be used to visualise the growth cycle of a *closed* culture in the laboratory.
The different phases of the curve shows the different stages of the population's growth.
![[Pasted image 20261005120425.png|536]]
The log phase (2) shows when there is an excess of nutrients and the bacterial population may grow unhindered. Eventually as nutrients are consumed and decrease, some cells begin to die off and the population reaches the stationary phase (3). When nutrients are too low to sustain the population more cells die than new cells are created and the population declines (4).

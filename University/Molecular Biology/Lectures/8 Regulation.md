Principles of regulation
Basic regulation of:
- DNA replication initiation
- mRNA transcription
- Translation of proteins
- Growth rate

Purpose:
- No phenotype can be optimal for all environments
- Stages of growth
- Respond to environmental changes
- Do not make what you do not need

Principles:
- Positive - activator
	Protein binds to DNA and transcription increases
- Negative - repressor
	Protein binds to DNA and transcription decreases
- Feedback - Product influences earlier steps, can be positive or negative
- Auto-regulation - The product of one gene can regulate its own expression
- Co-regulation - 2 genes regulated together
	Cis-acting vs. trans-acting

Hershey & Case:
Showed that DNA was responsible for traits being inherited.
Used virus to infect bacteria. Virus contained radioactive phosphor label. Compared to unlabelled virus. The viruses injected their genetic material in the bacteria. The bacteria replicated. After centrifugation the labelled bacteria were radioactive while the other was not. This showed that DNA was genetic material.

Genotype - genes
Phenotype - observable characteristics from specific genes, how the genes are expressed
Environment affects how the genes are expressed

Central dogma:
- Information from DNA to RNA to Protein only flows in one direction
- DNA replicates to more DNA
- DNA transcribes to RNA, RNA translates to protein

Chromosome:
*E. coli* chromosome is 1000 longer than the cell.
The DNA is coiled up in the cell to take less space
- Underwound - less than one turn per 10 base pairs, unwound
- Overwound - more than one turn per 10 base pairs

Topoisomerases change the supercoiling
- Class I: cut on strand, relax, reanneal. Does not use ATP
- Class II: cuts both strands, pass second DNA duplex through break, reanneal (requires ATP)
Chromatin packages DNA via binding proteins
- Eukaryotes - histones
- Prokaryotes - histone-like proteins
- DNA packaging can change gene-expression
- The influence of environmental factors on DNA packaging is **epigenetics**

DNA replication:
Conservative, semiconservative, and dispersive
DNA replication is semiconservative, only one strand from a pair is needed to create a new pair.

Plasmids:
Extrachromosomal genetic material that are not essential
Replicates separately from chromosome
Most circular or double-stranded
Advantageous but expendable functions such as antibiotic resistance or virulence 
Rolling circle replication
Plasmids with same oriC are incompatible

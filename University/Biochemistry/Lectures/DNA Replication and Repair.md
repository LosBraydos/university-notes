DNA to DNA
- Semiconservative: copy is more or less the same as original
- Initiation phase: assembly of replication (several enzymes/proteins)
- Elongation phase: new strand is synthesised (several enzymes)
- Termination phase: (several enzymes/binding proteins)

DNA Polymerase reaction is a phosphoryl transfer

Important proteins:
- Helicase = enzymes that separate DNA strands, requires ATP
- Topoisomerases = relieve topological stress created by strand separation
- DNA-binding proteins = stabilize separated strands, prevents degradation by nucleases
- Primases = Synthesize short segments of RNA to serve as primers
- DNA polymerase I = removes and replaces RNA primers
- RNase H1 = a specialized nuclease that degrades RNA in RNA-DNA hybrids, can remove RNA primers
- DNA ligases = seals nicks in the DNA backbone following removal and replacement of an RNA primer
